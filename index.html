HOW TO FLY & DOGFIGHT

1. Flight & Steering

Move your mouse/trackpad (or use WASD / Arrow Keys) to pitch and bank. Press L anytime to lock/unlock the cursor at center screen.

2. Guns & Homing Missiles

Spam-click or hold Left Click / C for rapid 20mm cannons (aim near the green lead circle). Press E / Right Click when a red LOCK diamond appears to launch homing missiles.

3. Afterburner & Barrel Roll

Hold Shift to ignite twin afterburners. Hold R to execute continuous barrel rolls and evade incoming enemy fire.

4. Hazards & Radar

Watch your altitude! Crashing into water, terrain, or city skyscrapers destroys your jet. Use the top-right Radar to spot red bandit triangles and orange city targets.
